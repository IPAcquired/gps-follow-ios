# Wired controller and XIAO lights

Open **Pi → Wired Desktop**. When the paired helper is attached, an idle Home or Pi page can open the viewer automatically. The Orange Pi's existing desktop account and review-before-apply workflow still apply. A new flight-safety condition, a lost heartbeat or a revoked session clears the viewer.

- Tap or drag in the desktop area. **Keyboard** opens a local keyboard over the lower part of the desktop. Tap **123**, **#+=**, **Nav** or **ABC** to cycle layouts. **Nav** includes arrows, Enter, Escape, Tab, Backspace, Delete and the supported clipboard shortcuts. The strip at the bottom stays local. **Hide** closes the keyboard and refreshes the covered rows. **Back** returns to the ordinary touchscreen menus.
- **Zoom** opens controls for 1×, 2× and 3×. Choose a magnification, then use the four pan buttons to move the visible part of the Pi desktop. **1× / Reset** restores the whole view. The Pi acknowledges a change once the new view is drawn. Touches during that transition stay local. To type, tap the destination first, then open **Keyboard**. If the Pi reports that focus changed, tap the intended field and reopen the keys.
- A dense scene can be received as a complete, bounded baseline JPEG. The panel updates it in a local burst after validating the complete image. Small changing rectangles can use lossless RGB565 tiles. The active helper path tries 500 kHz after an authenticated heartbeat and retries a bad physical frame, then falls back to 100 kHz after consecutive failures or stalled authenticated progress. Stable traffic allows a later retry at 500 kHz. The direct USB route uses the same encrypted pairing and input records.
- Open **Setup → Lights & Appearance → XIAO Lights** for the helper's addressable RGB and yellow indicator. The RGB choices are Off, Solid, Breathe, Rainbow, Beacon and Flashlight. Color, brightness, beacon pattern and speed correspond to the screen's existing controls. The yellow indicator offers Off, On, Follow RGB and Blink. The page updates after the helper acknowledges the actual settings and disables edits when it cannot confirm the helper. The XIAO retains these choices while it has power. The charge indicator is hardware controlled. **Back** returns to the screen's appearance options.
- The **Sense** page labels the configured CORVON 743 V2 and the Pi UART0 to flight controller UART4 connection. A fresh heartbeat reports link activity. The 360° C1 lidar on Pi UART2, 8×8 matrix on Pi UART7 and camera on Pi USB each show their own status.

The generic firmware contains no owner-specific DNS name or approved home network. The installer sets these identities once through the screen's local USB setup. Existing pairing and calibration are stored outside the application partition.
